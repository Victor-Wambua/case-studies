# Ozon Fix

**Status:** client project · **Built with:** WordPress, PHP, SEO

[← All case studies](../README.md)

## The problem

A commercial refrigeration and cooling firm needed enquiries it could track, and a site its staff could keep up to date without a developer.

## What it does

- A custom lead-generation theme covering commercial refrigeration, cold rooms, freezers, air conditioning, ventilation, ovens and microwaves.
- Each service has its own page, with a checklist, FAQs and a gallery.
- A **Request a Quote** form saves each request with a status, and sends the customer a confirmation.
- Settings screens for business details, the homepage, the quote form, and branding and tracking. Staff edit content and never touch the layout.
- Empty homepage sections hide themselves.
- Needs no plugins. A free SEO plugin is recommended for titles and sitemaps.

## How it was delivered

- A full handbook, a staff guide, and guides with screenshots for each role: owner, administrator, editor, sales and developer.
- Draft service pages and articles, and a change log.
